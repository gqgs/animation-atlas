KALEIDOSCOPE / DIHEDRAL SYMMETRY

Inside the chamber

Inspired by Greg Egan’s Kaleidoscope (2000). A collection of colored regular polygons drifts and turns between two mirrors separated by π/n. Clipping to that wedge and repeating its reflection creates 2n sectors, with n-fold rotational symmetry.

The polygons move on closed elliptical paths. Each turns by one vertex spacing per loop, so the final arrangement equals the first. Shapes crossing a mirror merge with their reflections; nothing is simulated outside the original wedge.

This independent implementation adds continuous motion, seeded arrangements, palettes, a timeline and capture controls. The seed and controls are stored in the scene link. Loading a link restores a paused frame. Record loop saves a real-time WebM at a 30 fps target; performance depends on your device.
