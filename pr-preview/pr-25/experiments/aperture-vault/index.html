How the light works

Six rigid triangular faces close at one apex. A triangular glass prism travels with one face. Near closure it crosses the white beam and refracts the wavelengths at two nonparallel interfaces. The soft halos visualize the computed paths; they are not volumetric simulation. Both renderers use the same optics.
