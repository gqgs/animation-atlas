How the light works

Six rigid triangular faces close at one apex. Rectangular light sources mirror across the moving faces and brighten at grazing angles. A representative reflected ray per face and source places soft light on the floor. Both renderers use the same approximation. The core’s color and intensity control its luminous body, nearby illumination and contribution to the floor reflections; the studio strips stay fixed.
