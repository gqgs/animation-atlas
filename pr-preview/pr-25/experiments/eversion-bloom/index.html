The construction

A continuous annular shell rolls its rim past vertical. Two counter-traveling waves carry the reversal around seven fluted lobes. Teal is one material side; plum-copper is the other. The camera stays fixed by default.

Reversal controls the curl. Pleat relief shapes the fine radial folds. This is a stylized surface inversion, not rigid origami or a topological sphere eversion. The sheet stretches. A depth buffer resolves visibility; the shape does not depend on self-intersection.
