The mathematics & numerical limits

Every point is the complex integral Ψ(x,y,z) = ∬ exp(iΦ) ds dt, with

Φ = s³ − 3st² + z(s² + t²) + xs + yt.

The gold caustic marks where stationary points merge. Rings mark isolated zeros of Ψ; phase is undefined there. At z = 0 the integral is real and nodal curves replace isolated phase vortices.

Time sweeps a parameter; this is not a simulation of physical time. Negative z is obtained by complex conjugation. Color mapping stays fixed.

Precomputed 231 × 231 complex samples, Δz = 0.025, finite smooth aperture; interpolation in the complex field, never in hue. Zero markers are grid-resolved estimates and can miss close pairs. Fine fringes and the phase near zeros are resolution-limited. Intensity is |Ψ|² with fixed exposure tone mapping.
