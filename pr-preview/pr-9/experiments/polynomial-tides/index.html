What is this?

Each seed identifies twelve coefficients for Sprott's quadratic map. The field is drawn from actual iterates. Cached filaments gently drift and brighten with continuous closed-loop motion; the underlying recurrence never changes.

The original search requires bounded orbits, positive finite-time stretching and spatial coverage. Shape search also admits near-neutral Lyapunov exponents, ranks confirmed orbits by covariance isotropy, radial consistency and rotational similarity (2–8 fold), and offers the best eight results. Metrics describe the actual sampled orbit, not the rendered drift. These are heuristics, not mathematical proofs of chaos, exact symmetry, or historical novelty.

Based on Paul Bourke's polynomial attractors and J. C. Sprott's 1993 paper. The exported MP4 samples the scene at fixed timestamps. Filaments are cached once per resolution and control change; frames only composite the cached images. Dragging sliders avoids costly repeated rebuilds.
