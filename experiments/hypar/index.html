The construction

Eight square-pleated saddles twist, open and rise as one wave travels around the ring. The camera is stationary by default. Wave strength controls the deformation; set it to zero to study the folds.

Inspired by Demaine, Demaine & Lubiw’s pleated hypars and MIT 6.849. This is a stylized mathematical surface, not a validated rigid origami simulation. Sheets are separate; edge lengths are not preserved.
