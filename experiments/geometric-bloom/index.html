The construction

A hexagonal pyramid cut along its apex edges. Six rigid triangular faces pivot about the uncut base edges. Opening 0 closes the pyramid; 1 lays its connected net flat. No stretching or vertex morphing. Crystal height changes the source shape, not its folding motion.

This explicit, outward-opening construction is inspired by continuous blooming and MIT's geometric folding course. It is not an implementation of the general unfolding algorithm.
