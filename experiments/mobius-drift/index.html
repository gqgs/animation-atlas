The map beneath the motion

Greg Egan’s Schwarz (1998) pulls a decorated square tiling back through two complex transformations:

f(z) = 1 + (z − z₀)/(1 − z̄₀z)
g(w) = (w + 1)/(w − 1)

The composition simplifies to g(f(z)) = 1 + 2(1 − z̄₀z)/(z − z₀). Each pixel samples the tiling at that transformed coordinate. The pole z₀ maps to infinity; everything nearby is compressed into finer and finer tiles.

Here z₀ moves continuously. The default orbit takes two minutes. Integer Lissajous frequencies make a closed, repeatable path. Concentric and radial decorations follow Egan’s description; spiral rosettes and palettes are our extensions.

Path centers and amplitudes are scaled together when needed to keep |z₀| below 0.96. Pattern detail fades toward its mean as it becomes smaller than a pixel, reducing shimmer near the pole. This is filtered visualization, not infinite resolution.
